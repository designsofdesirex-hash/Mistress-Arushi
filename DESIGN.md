# Mistress Arushi visual system

## Direction

An image-led fashion editorial for a genuine young Queen. The design earns its identity through Arushi's photography, a distinctive plum-and-cream atmosphere, elegant type proportions, and a direct contact path. It contains no fake dossier, case-file, numbered-section, ticker, or technical-dashboard language.

## Palette and type

Deep plum `#24151d` is the main evening surface; cream `#f3ebdf` creates the quiet editorial pause; coral `#ef765f` owns the proof field; peach `#ffb36b` marks action; periwinkle `#7f8dff` is a secondary action. Bodoni Moda is the display face; Plus Jakarta Sans handles all navigation, body copy, and controls.

## Composition

The first screen is an oversized promise paired with one tall organic portrait crop. The manifesto is a single generous text field. Services are three large open rows, not cards. Proof is an image/video-led color break. The vault is an asymmetric, organically cropped image field. Contact is one direct two-channel close.

## Interaction and responsive rules

Buttons are substantial, full-width on small screens, and respond with a short lift and press scale. Image hover uses a restrained settle. Reduced motion removes transforms. The desktop hero is two columns; mobile becomes a clean vertical reading path with no decorative side rails.

The booking form sits in the contact close after the proof and before the media vault in the visitor's reading path. It uses three short fields, one copy action, and two direct social actions; it does not introduce a backend, account flow, or pricing grid.

## Content boundaries

English is default and Hindi persists through `localStorage`. Official routes are Telegram `arushii_sharma00` and X `MistressArushi`. Supplied photo and verification video assets are used. No prices, testimonials, payment handles, or unsupported claims are fabricated.
